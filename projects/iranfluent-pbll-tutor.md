# IranFluent AI PBLL Project Tutor — Stage A Concierge Pilot

AI-facilitated Project-Based Language Learning product for Persian-speaking English learners, built on the 12-year IranFluent business. Stage A is a zero-code instrumented concierge pilot: Hiwa facilitates manually in a private Telegram group, AI assists behind the scenes, every exchange stage-tagged and logged as the Stage B spec + moat-data seed.

## Key decisions

- **Approach:** B — instrumented concierge (A → B staged; bot only after Stage A passes)
- **All 6 cherry-picks accepted:** WooCommerce checkout synergy, showcase channel as marketing engine, season model pre-commitment, before/after evidence capture, error corpus capture, NFLRC visibility play
- **Artifact type (resolved):** spoken video/voice story — "In 4 weeks you publish your first English voice/video story to a real audience"
- **Refund policy:** full refund during week 1; none after
- **Silent-student protocol:** 48h nudge → 48h cutoff → mark inactive
- **Weekly group session:** 45-min hard stop (10 showcase / 20 NTK wall review / 15 Q&A), recorded
- **Data storage:** private spreadsheet — session log tab (stage tag, error type, before/after notes) + cohort summary tab (stage, last activity, status)
- **Week-2 completion pulse:** <60% submitted NTK wall + one draft → trigger overload rule before week 3
- **Stage B trigger:** design begins the week after the Stage A showcase event

## Two-gate validation

- **Gate 1:** ONE Persian paragraph offer → 17 personal DMs to named past buyers (Vahid, Hoshang, Alireza first). ≥3 paid deposits within 1 week.
- **Gate 2:** offer to 20k email list, 2-week window. ≥10 paid total.
- **Kill rule:** either gate fails → Stage A cancelled, $750 + attention go to WooCommerce checkout repair.

## Stage A success criteria

≥60% complete, ≥50% publish, ≥40% exit-survey "would buy AI-run version", ≥2 actual Season 2 rebuys. Founder time capped at 10h/week.

## The Assignment (72h from 2026-07-22)

Write the pilot offer paragraph (4 weeks, named outcome, real Toman price, start date, consent line, week-1 refund window) and send to the 17 named past buyers. Report back replies/yes/paid. No building, no curriculum, no bot until those replies land.
